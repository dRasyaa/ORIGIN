The story behind the bowl

Good food should feel good in every way.

Kapaw! began with a simple thought: our cats deserve thoughtful nutrition, and the ingredients around us deserve to be valued.

We bring these two ideas together through human-grade processing standards, carefully selected local ingredients, and recipes that take inspiration from Indonesia's culinary heritage.
